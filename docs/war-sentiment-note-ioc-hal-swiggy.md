## 2. Key Macro & War Transmission Channels

### 2.1 Oil Price Shock
- Every $10/bbl increase in crude costs Indian OMCs (IOC, BPCL, HPCL) an estimated **₹8,000–12,000 crore/year in additional input costs** (before government under-recovery adjustments).
- If retail fuel prices are capped by GoI for political reasons, marketing margins collapse → earnings risk for IOC is asymmetric to the downside during escalation.
- Brent above $100 increases India's import bill by ~$12–15 bn/year, widening the **current account deficit** and pressuring INR.

### 2.2 INR / Currency Channel
- A weaker INR (₹86–88 range vs. USD) increases crude import costs in rupee terms even if dollar-denominated crude is flat.
- Defense imports (components for HAL's platforms) become costlier; however, HAL's domestic content is rising (~60% indigenization), partially insulating margins.
- Swiggy's dollar-denominated cloud infrastructure costs (AWS/GCP) and any USD-linked vendor contracts become more expensive.

### 2.3 Interest Rate Channel
- RBI faces a stagflation dilemma: cut rates to support growth or hold to defend INR and fight imported inflation.
- Higher-for-longer rates compress valuations of **loss-making or thin-margin growth names** like Swiggy (high EV/Revenue multiples sensitive to discount rate).
- IOC's heavy debt load (~₹80,000+ crore gross debt historically) increases finance costs in a rising-rate environment.

### 2.4 Defense Budget Expansion
- Conflict escalation globally, combined with India's own border tensions, creates a structural tailwind for defense capex.
- India's defense budget is expected to remain elevated (>2% of GDP); HAL's order backlog (₹90,000+ crore range) provides multi-year revenue visibility.
- "Make in India" defense policy accelerates indigenization timelines → HAL is a direct beneficiary.

### 2.5 Supply Chain Disruption
- Hormuz disruption affects crude tanker routes → IOC's procurement costs rise; refinery run-rates may be impacted if crude availability tightens.
- Titanium, aluminum alloys, and specialty electronics used in HAL platforms: India's diversification away from Russian supply chains is ongoing but not complete.
- Swiggy's supply chain (packaging, fuel for delivery, cold chain logistics) faces inflation from elevated oil prices → delivery economics worsen.
